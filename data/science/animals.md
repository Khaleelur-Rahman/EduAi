# Animals and Their Habitats

## What are Animals?
Animals are living things that can move, eat food, and have babies. Unlike plants, animals cannot make their own food, so they need to eat other living things.

## Types of Animals
### Mammals
- Have fur or hair
- Feed their babies with milk
- Examples: dogs, cats, elephants, whales, humans

### Birds
- Have feathers and wings
- Most can fly
- Lay eggs with hard shells
- Examples: eagles, penguins, robins, ostriches

### Fish
- Live in water
- Have gills to breathe underwater
- Have scales covering their bodies
- Examples: goldfish, sharks, salmon, clownfish

### Reptiles
- Have scales or bony plates
- Are cold-blooded (body temperature changes with environment)
- Examples: snakes, lizards, turtles, crocodiles

### Amphibians
- Live both in water and on land
- Have smooth, moist skin
- Examples: frogs, toads, salamanders

## Animal Habitats
Different animals live in different places called habitats:

- **Forest**: Trees, squirrels, bears, birds
- **Ocean**: Fish, whales, dolphins, sharks
- **Desert**: Camels, snakes, lizards, cacti
- **Arctic**: Polar bears, penguins, seals, walruses
- **Grassland**: Lions, zebras, elephants, giraffes

## How Animals Survive
- **Camouflage**: Some animals blend in with their surroundings to hide from predators
- **Migration**: Some animals travel long distances to find food or warmer weather
- **Hibernation**: Some animals sleep through the winter when food is scarce
- **Adaptation**: Animals develop special features to help them survive in their environment

## Fun Facts About Animals
- A group of lions is called a pride
- Elephants can remember things for many years
- Dolphins have names for each other
- Some birds can fly for months without landing
- Octopuses have three hearts and blue blood
