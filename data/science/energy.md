# Energy and How Things Work

## What is Energy?
Energy is what makes things happen! It's the ability to do work or make changes. Everything around us needs energy to work.

## Types of Energy
### Light Energy
- Comes from the Sun and light bulbs
- Helps plants grow
- Allows us to see things
- Can be converted to electricity with solar panels

### Heat Energy
- Makes things warm or hot
- Comes from the Sun, fire, or friction
- Used for cooking, heating our homes, and keeping us warm

### Motion Energy (Kinetic Energy)
- Energy of moving things
- A rolling ball, running person, or spinning wheel
- The faster something moves, the more motion energy it has

### Sound Energy
- Energy that travels through air as sound waves
- Made when things vibrate
- Allows us to hear music, voices, and other sounds

### Electrical Energy
- Energy that flows through wires
- Powers our lights, computers, and appliances
- Can be made from other types of energy

## Energy Sources
### Renewable Energy (Can be used again and again)
- **Solar**: Energy from the Sun
- **Wind**: Energy from moving air
- **Water**: Energy from flowing water
- **Geothermal**: Energy from heat inside the Earth

### Non-Renewable Energy (Will run out someday)
- **Coal**: Burned to make electricity
- **Oil**: Used for cars and heating
- **Natural Gas**: Used for cooking and heating

## How Energy Changes
Energy can change from one type to another:
- Sunlight (light energy) → Plants grow (chemical energy)
- Food (chemical energy) → Our bodies move (motion energy)
- Wind (motion energy) → Windmill spins (motion energy) → Electricity (electrical energy)

## Saving Energy
We can save energy by:
- Turning off lights when we leave a room
- Walking or biking instead of driving
- Using energy-efficient light bulbs
- Not wasting water
- Recycling materials

## Fun Facts About Energy
- Lightning is a huge electrical discharge
- The human body uses energy even when sleeping
- A single wind turbine can power hundreds of homes
- Solar panels can work even on cloudy days
- Energy cannot be created or destroyed, only changed from one form to another
