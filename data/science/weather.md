# Weather and Climate

## What is Weather?
Weather is what the air is like outside right now. It changes every day and includes things like temperature, rain, wind, and clouds.

## Weather Elements
### Temperature
- How hot or cold it is
- Measured with a thermometer
- Measured in degrees (Fahrenheit or Celsius)

### Precipitation
- Water falling from the sky
- **Rain**: Liquid water drops
- **Snow**: Frozen water crystals
- **Hail**: Ice balls that form in thunderstorms
- **Sleet**: Rain that freezes before hitting the ground

### Wind
- Moving air
- Can be gentle breezes or strong gusts
- Measured with an anemometer
- Wind direction tells us where it's coming from

### Clouds
- Collections of tiny water droplets or ice crystals
- **Cumulus**: Puffy white clouds (fair weather)
- **Stratus**: Flat, gray clouds (often bring rain)
- **Cirrus**: Wispy, high clouds (made of ice crystals)
- **Cumulonimbus**: Tall, dark clouds (thunderstorms)

## The Water Cycle
Water moves in a continuous cycle:
1. **Evaporation**: Sun heats water, turning it into water vapor
2. **Condensation**: Water vapor cools and forms clouds
3. **Precipitation**: Water falls back to Earth as rain, snow, etc.
4. **Collection**: Water flows into rivers, lakes, and oceans

## Types of Weather
### Sunny Days
- Clear skies with lots of sunshine
- Usually warm and pleasant
- Good for outdoor activities

### Rainy Days
- Water falls from clouds
- Can be light drizzle or heavy downpour
- Important for plants and animals

### Snowy Days
- Frozen precipitation falls from clouds
- Happens when it's very cold
- Can create beautiful winter scenes

### Stormy Weather
- Thunder and lightning
- Heavy rain and strong winds
- Can be dangerous, so stay inside

## Weather Tools
- **Thermometer**: Measures temperature
- **Rain Gauge**: Measures how much rain fell
- **Wind Vane**: Shows wind direction
- **Barometer**: Measures air pressure

## Fun Facts About Weather
- Lightning can heat the air to 50,000°F (hotter than the Sun's surface)
- A single raindrop can contain millions of water molecules
- Snowflakes are always six-sided, but no two are exactly alike
- The windiest place on Earth is Antarctica
- Thunder is the sound of air expanding rapidly due to lightning's heat
